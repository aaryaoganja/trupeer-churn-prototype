    NewProduct rule: "Suited to this change" appears on Update when under 60% of the asset changed, otherwise on Recreate. The 60% threshold is a starting point to calibrate, not a finding.

    ExistingScript and voiceover edits already cost 0 credits (observed on the trial). This comparison is for when footage changes, where the trial offers no step-level replacement: only re-record and regenerate. Generating a 5:43 video + guide used 600 credits.
    Pricing policyMetering on the changed segment is a proposed policy. The recreate figure uses Trupeer's published rates. How swap is billed on Enterprise is unverified.
